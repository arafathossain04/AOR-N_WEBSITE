When you send us a message through the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, brand or company (optional) and your message. If you contact us on WhatsApp, Instagram or LinkedIn, we receive whatever you send us on those platforms.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as needed to respond and to keep business records, then delete them when they are no longer needed.
